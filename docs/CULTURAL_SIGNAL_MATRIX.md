# Cultural Signal Matrix

Pantone Challenger is an open observatory for how color begins, spreads, converges, and becomes ordinary across culture.

## Two axes for every observation

Every source observation must declare both:

1. **Domain** — where the color appeared: art, fashion, marketplaces, advertising, design and interiors, entertainment, technology, creator and indie culture, audience intent, materials and production, public visual field, or lifestyle.
2. **Signal stage** — what the observation means: creation, distribution, or attention.

Additional required fields are scale class, panel type, event type, geography, platform, rights mode, and observation timestamp.

## Panels

- **Benchmark panel:** stable sources measured consistently for historical comparison.
- **Discovery panel:** rotating sources intended to find small, early, and unusual signals.

The panels are scored separately before convergence. A large platform cannot overwhelm the discovery panel, and a tiny source cannot manufacture a mainstream trend.

## Outputs

- **Undercurrent:** strongest early signal among independent and discovery sources.
- **Challenger:** strongest emerging color supported across unrelated domains and/or stages.
- **Mainstream leader:** broadest current usage, whether or not it is new.
- **Raw usage leader:** highest unadjusted prevalence, retained for audit only.

A day can have two co-Challengers. A frequently co-occurring pair is reported as a palette pairing, not mislabeled as a tie.

## Palette regimes

The system also measures neutral share, median chroma, lightness, warm/cool balance, contrast, palette size, monochrome share, muted/electric share, and recurring color pairs. Monthly and annual reports can therefore describe broader visual regimes rather than reducing culture to one HEX value.

## Guardrails

- One independent actor receives one normalized vote per candidate.
- One platform and one domain are capped.
- Logos, favicons, navigation, cookie notices, verification screens, and page chrome cannot cast color votes.
- Full creative images remain private when rights mode is `analyze_only`; public outputs use swatches and attribution.
- The machine selects the color. Narrative systems may explain it but cannot alter the ranking.
