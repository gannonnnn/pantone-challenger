# Social Account and Historical Archive

## Launch posture

Start with manual review and manual posting. Automatic publishing remains off until a sustained calibration period is complete.

## Public identity

**Pantone Challenger**  
**The Open Cultural Color Index**

Suggested concise description:

> Small signals. Big signals. What color is moving through culture now? An independent art-tech project; not affiliated with Pantone.

## Daily package

The feed card displays the Challenger or co-Challengers. Stories provide:

1. winner/tie and exact swatches;
2. traceable source evidence and domains;
3. why the signal qualified;
4. runner-ups;
5. Undercurrent, Mainstream leader, and palette-pair context.

Every package includes captions and alt text.

## Historical page

GitHub Pages builds a static archive from approved results on `main`. It includes:

- a chronological color grid;
- daily detail pages;
- ties and palette pairings;
- recurrence and streaks;
- source coverage;
- domain/stage/scale breakdowns;
- palette-regime summaries;
- annual reports;
- methodology and source policy.

The site is deliberately static: it is inexpensive, inspectable, and can later move behind a custom domain.

## Approval rules

- Calibration and review-only days are not public social claims.
- Baseline-only days teach the model what normal looks like but do not create a Challenger.
- A result becomes official only after its pull request is reviewed and merged.
- Reverted results do not count toward recurrence or annual statistics.
